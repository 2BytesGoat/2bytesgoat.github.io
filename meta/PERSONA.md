Not "a game with a TCP API." Three threads landing in one place:

## GenAI stance (on the record: skeptic with taste)

Caught in the middle, and that's the differentiator — he builds _with_ AI, mocks the hype, and empathizes with the artists hurt by it:

- Against: speed-pressure culture ("no patience to think things through"), shiny-thing FOMO, snake-oil vendors, "AI in their freaking coffee" people, the 10-subscriptions-100-agents-and-he's-the-CEO hive mind
- Empathizes with: creative-community resentment — genAI as "the thing that stole their craft"
- His own use: pragmatic ("I know how to vibecode it" — fast at work, saved time goes into the game)
- **Voice implication**: jokes about hype are always welcome; contempt is not. Punch at the _culture_, not at people exploring tools.
