_Mock interview. Real answers. More coming._

**You said you don't even want to press the buttons anymore. What does that mean?**

It's like a bad Scooby Doo scene where you run from the monster and the monster was just a random dude. It's unjustified fear. I can't point it out.

**When was the last time that happened?**

I tried at some point, 3 weeks ago during a game jam, and I was like _I moved too fast, idk where I was, idk how this thing works, people are looking, mom pick me up._

**Who makes you feel like that?**

It's this dude from work. He feels like a fucking god amongst men. He lives and breathes code — or at least I think that's the persona he shows — but like, every time he looks at code the dude's laser focused, and with AI he's a fucking machine.

**So you tried to prove you could do it without AI. What happened?**

At the start of the year I fucked my brains building Shaman Arena. Like, literally only used AI instead of googling, but implemented everything from scratch. Day and night, 2 days straight. The code was jank af but the product was golden.

And then when I started showing people, they were like:

> "you made it with AI right?"
> "like it's not possible you did it without"
> "oh, you're serious"
> "but why tho?"

**Before AI, what made you feel like you'd done a real day's work?**

When I was working at a previous job, trying to do like literally a one-man-army thing — designing all the code myself, talking to everyone, setting deadlines — I WAS FUCKING DEPLETED. And then after work I had to work on my game and TAGAP, and there was literally no time for my wife and my life. Like I needed to give myself all for the craft.

Then when I switched jobs, and people were on such a freaking level and I had massive impostor syndrome, that I literally had to get juiced up to compete with them.

**So is any of this actually worse?**

Before AI I felt like my code was shit, and I was tired all the time. Now the code is better, I feel better designing stuff, but I still ain't happy.

So idk what golden geese I'm chasing actually.

**So is AI good or bad for you?**

AI makes or breaks you. It's a tool that gives you fucking speed. But same as fucking roids, you need to understand that you will go fast, but you'll freaking lose yourself if you go too fast.

**And the people who don't want to use it?**

Listen, it's your choice not to use it, and the way it got to this point is absolutely bullcrap and it shouldn't be this way. But same for Google and Microsoft and your fucking phone. The damage is already done, people suffered for that shit and not using it won't undo the suffering. So at least we could just use it and try to make good with it.
